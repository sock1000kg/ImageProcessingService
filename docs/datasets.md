# Datasets and what we may do with them

Every dataset the project uses, plans to use, or ruled out, with the usage terms **as checked on 2026-11-12**. "Checked" means I read the
source named in the Terms column; "not verified" means I could not reach or read it, so do not rely on the entry. The project is
non-commercial (school, open source). This is a record of what the sources say, not legal advice. The images of every dataset stay out of git
(`datasets/`, `images*/` are gitignored); only fetch scripts, lists and manifests are committed. Where each dataset and its labels can be found: [Where the data lives](#where-the-data-lives).

| **BDD100K** (Berkeley DeepDrive, 100K images, 1280x720 US dashcam) | Plate detector fine-tune: 250 frames with small, distant and night plates (`engine/training/datasets/dashcam-plates/`) | 1. Steps 1–4 were run by hand, not by a script; their lists are kept locally in `engine/training/datasets/bdd100k/` (`pool_list.txt`, `pool_vehicles.json`, `sample_list.txt`, `sample_preds.json`). The 250 frames used are listed in `engine/training/detection/lists/bdd100k-frames.txt`, and `engine/training/detection/fetch_extras.py bdd100k` downloads them again (byte-identical, checked 2026-11-21). 5. Pre-labelled with `yolo26s-1280-e5-plates-frozen-x3` (plates at confidence >= 0.08, faces >= 0.25), then corrected by hand in CVAT: every plate recognisable as one, readable or not, boxed on its visible part; wrong boxes deleted. 6. Converted to YOLO (class 0 face, 1 plate): 292 plates, 42 faces | Checked 2026-11-18: the licence notice in the [Hugging Face mirror](https://huggingface.co/datasets/karthik789338/BDD100K-Images) (`LICENSE_NOTICE.md`, © 2018 The Regents of the University of California) allows use, copying, changes and distribution **for educational, research and not-for-profit purposes**, with the notice kept; commercial use needs a licence from UC Berkeley. The mirror is **unofficial** (uploaded for the OccDet-Calib project, no licence field on its card); the official download needs registration at [bdd-data.berkeley.edu](http://bdd-data.berkeley.edu). Keep images local, cite: Yu et al., *BDD100K: A Diverse Driving Dataset for Heterogeneous Multitask Learning*, CVPR 2020 | Used, local only |

The 384 photos used, with author and source page for the credit, are in `engine/training/detection/lists/gauge-negatives.csv`, and `engine/training/detection/fetch_extras.py gauges` downloads them again with their empty labels (byte-identical, checked 2026-11-21). Mostly car dashboards, clocks and dial gauges, few motorbike clusters | Checked 2026-11-19 on the [Open Images site](https://storage.googleapis.com/openimages/web/factsfigures_v7.html): images "listed as having a CC BY 2.0 license" (all 1,468 rows here say CC BY 2.0; Google asks to verify per image), annotations CC BY 4.0. ## Looked at and not used

| Dataset | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| Roboflow Universe Vietnamese plate sets | One plate per full-scene image, so nothing new over UET; licences vary per project (for example public domain and CC BY 4.0 on two of them) |
| CCPD | Chinese plates, one per image, parking-lot scenes |
| [Da Nang CCTV vehicle dataset](https://www.techscience.com/cmc/v88n1/67301) | Vehicles, pedestrians and traffic lights only (no plates or faces); the article gives no download link or data terms. The CC BY 4.0 notice is the article's, not necessarily the data's |

## When adding a dataset

Add a row first: what it is used for, how we prepared it (downloads, filtering, conversion, labelling, counts), the terms with the URL you read them at, and whether images may be committed (default: no).
